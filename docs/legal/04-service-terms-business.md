## The regulated-activity boundary

The single most important section in this document, and the one with the clearest
downside if it is wrong.

Business Builder coordinates company formation. It does not perform it. The
distinction is not cosmetic. In Texas, giving advice on entity selection or
preparing legal documents for another person can constitute the unauthorised
practice of law, which is a criminal offence and also unwinds the commercial
position entirely.

### Permitted framing

Prepare. Guide. Coordinate. Prefill where permitted. Track. Connect. Verify
completion. Explain what a step is and why it exists. Tell the customer what they
need to decide.

### Prohibited framing

Do not say, in any customer-facing material or contract:

- "We form your LLC"
- "We file your formation documents"
- "We get your EIN"
- "We open your bank account"
- "We register your business"
- "We handle the legal side"
- Any recommendation of one entity type over another for this customer's situation
- Any statement that a chosen structure is correct, optimal, or compliant

`[COUNSEL]` Review the Texas unauthorised practice of law position directly,
including the statutory software and forms exemption and what it does and does not
cover. The exemption is narrow and depends on clear disclosure that the product is
not a substitute for an attorney. Determine what disclosure is required and where it
must appear.

`[COUNSEL]` Determine whether selecting an entity type for a customer, as distinct
from explaining the options, can be done at all without a licensed professional,
and if not, how the product presents the choice. This affects product design, not
only wording, and should be resolved before the first business build.

`[COUNSEL]` Confirm the same analysis for tax matters. Advising on tax elections is
a separate regulated activity from legal advice and the boundary is different.

## The Business Opportunity Rule

Not previously considered in this package and it belongs here, because it attaches to
this offer specifically rather than to the company generally.

There are two rules, one federal and one Texas, and they trip on different things.
Full findings and citations are in
[25-texas-consumer-privacy-upl.md](25-texas-consumer-privacy-upl.md), sections 1 and 2.

### The federal rule, which is broader than it first appears

16 CFR part 437 requires three elements: solicitation into a **new business**, a
required payment with **no dollar floor**, and a representation that the seller will
provide outlets, accounts **or customers**, including internet customers, for the
purchaser's goods or services.

**No earnings claim is required.** The customer-provision prong stands alone, and a
further provision extends it to "**otherwise assisting the prospective purchaser in
obtaining his or her own … customers**."

That phrase is the difficulty. Lead capture, local presence, listings, Google Business
Profile setup and an AI worker whose stated job is handling inbound enquiries are all
plausibly assistance in obtaining the purchaser's own customers.

**The disclosure must be delivered at least seven calendar days before the buyer signs
or pays.** That is incompatible with same-day web checkout, which is the direction the
intake flow is being built.

An enforcement action settled in March 2026 against an AI company selling to small
businesses ended in an $18 million judgment and a **permanent ban on marketing business
opportunities**. Three comparable matters ended the same way.

### The Texas Act, which needs both elements

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 51 defines a business opportunity as a sale of services over
**$500** used to begin a business where the seller represents **both** that the
purchaser is likely to earn a profit exceeding what they paid, **and** that the seller
will provide a sales, production or marketing program.

**The second element is satisfied outright.** The first is entirely within Business
Builder's control, and the site currently makes no profit representation.

If both trip: a disclosure statement filed with the Secretary of State **before
advertising or offering**, a **$25,000** bond or equivalent, a prohibition on income
representations without substantiating data disclosed at the time, per se DTPA
liability for any violation, and a statutory provision making any waiver void.

### What follows

**The safe posture is the one the site already largely holds.** No representation that
the business will get customers, accounts or revenue, and no earnings claims anywhere.
Two places drift toward it, both flagged in
[20-website-copy-audit.md](20-website-copy-audit.md): the search ranking claim, and the
statement that founders start taking customers while the remaining work finishes.

`[COUNSEL]` **This is the most urgent single question in the package.** It affects
whether the offer can be sold on a same-day checkout at all. Both obligations attach
before the sale, so neither can be satisfied retroactively.

`[COUNSEL]` The **Franchise Rule** needs trademark association, significant control or
assistance, and a required payment. The second and third are satisfied. The first is
controllable: if customers trade under their own name and never hold out as associated
with a Business Builder mark, there is no franchise.

### The three-day cancellation law, which reaches the pilot

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 601 covers consumer transactions over **$25** where the
merchant personally solicited **away from its own place of business** and the agreement
was signed away from it.

**Signing a customer at their own premises triggers it.** It requires a boldface notice
of at least 10 points adjacent to the signature line, in prescribed words, plus a
detachable duplicate cancellation form. **A non-compliant contract is void**, and
non-compliance is a per se DTPA violation.

`[COUNSEL]` The pilot agreement is likely to be signed at the cleaning company's
premises. Either sign at a fixed business location, or comply. Cheap to get right, fatal
to get wrong. See [07-pilot-service-agreement.md](07-pilot-service-agreement.md).

## Unauthorised practice of law, worked out

The full analysis with authorities is in
[25-texas-consumer-privacy-upl.md](25-texas-consumer-privacy-upl.md), section 6. Four
points that change how this service must be built.

**The statutory safe harbour protects the product, not the conversation.** It covers
the design, creation, publication, distribution, display or sale of forms and software
that clearly state they are not a substitute for an attorney. Six verbs. **"Select",
"customise", "apply" and "advise" are not among them.**

**The controlling case draws the line inside form-filling.** Recording a client's own
answers onto a form probably does not require legal skill. **Determining whether the
form should be filed at all does.** Selecting and preparing forms was held to be the
practice of law.

**A disclaimer does not defeat implied advice.** A case decided after the safe harbour
took effect held that in preparing documents, the company **impliedly advised** its
clients of their legal rights. The position "we never gave advice" is not available
when the conduct implies it.

**"Free" does not help.** A service given without separate charge as an inducement is
still part of what the customer pays for. A free operating agreement bundled with every
formation package is the exact fact pattern rejected.

### Applied

| Activity | Position |
|---|---|
| Recording the founder's answers and filing | Permissible |
| **Selecting entity type** | Practice of law |
| Drafting or customising an operating agreement | Practice of law. Publishing a disclaimered template is the protected path |
| "Which is better for me" | Practice of law |

`[COUNSEL]` **Texas has no document-preparer licensing lane**, unlike several other
states. The question is binary and the statutory safe harbour is the only shelter.

`[COUNSEL]` **Holding out as a lawyer with intent to obtain economic benefit is a
third-degree felony under the Penal Code.** The exposure is in how the service is
described, not only in what it does.

`[COUNSEL]` **The S-corporation election question** sits at the intersection of Texas
UPL and federal practice before the IRS. The employer identification application is
exempt from the federal preparer requirement. The election form is not. This is the
single highest-risk question in a standard formation funnel.

`[COUNSEL]` If a notary is ever on staff for formation or banking paperwork, a specific
Texas statute criminalises accepting compensation to obtain relief from a state or
federal agency, and makes non-compliance a deceptive trade practice.

**One honest gap.** No Texas appellate decision squarely addresses non-lawyer entity
formation or entity selection. The analysis above is analogy from immigration forms,
wills and title work. The analogies are strong and consistent, and none of them is the
answer.

## Founder Actions

Certain steps cannot be delegated. The product already treats these as Founder
Actions and the contract must reflect it in absolute terms.

A step must be performed by the founder personally where it requires:

- Personal signature
- Identity verification
- Regulated acceptance, such as accepting a provider's terms as principal
- Any action a provider or authority requires the principal to take personally

Business Builder prepares the step, explains it, and records its completion.
Business Builder does not perform it, and will not impersonate the customer to any
provider or authority under any circumstances.

`[COUNSEL]` Confirm the list is complete and whether any item carries a specific
statutory prohibition rather than a provider requirement.

## No guarantee of external approval

Business Builder cannot guarantee that any external authority or provider will
approve anything. Not the Secretary of State, not the IRS, not a bank, not an
insurer, not a payment processor, not a listing platform.

Required contract position:

> Business Builder does not guarantee approval, acceptance or registration by any
> government agency, bank, insurer, payment processor, listing platform or other
> third party. Where an application is rejected, Business Builder will tell Customer
> why if the reason is disclosed, and will assist Customer in responding. Fees paid
> to a third party are not refundable by Business Builder.

`[COUNSEL]` Decide what happens when a filing is rejected for a reason attributable
to Business Builder's preparation. Correcting and resubmitting at no charge, with
the customer bearing only the refiling fee, is the fair position and should be
stated rather than left to argument.

## Scope

From the site copy, now contractual:

- Starting-point intake, research and a recommendation the customer approves first
- Company foundation steps prepared, tracked and verified
- Identity: name direction, positioning, brand, domain, email, business phone
- Customer system: website, lead capture, CRM, scheduling, payments
- Operations: workflows, policies, quote terms, bookkeeping and document setup
- Local presence: Google Business Profile, listings, local search, analytics
- Founder Actions prepared, explained and captured
- Continuous verification, Ready and Fully Set, then handoff

`[COUNSEL]` "Bookkeeping and document setup" needs care. Setting up a bookkeeping
tool is configuration. Performing bookkeeping or advising on treatment is not, and
the line should be stated in the scope rather than assumed.

`[COUNSEL]` "Name direction" must not become a trademark clearance opinion. Business
Builder should state expressly that it does not perform trademark searches or
provide clearance, and that the customer should obtain advice before committing to a
name. This is a real and commonly overlooked exposure: a customer who builds a brand
on an infringing name has a large loss and an obvious person to blame.

## Ready and Fully Set

The product defines two states and the contract references them, which makes their
definitions contractual.

`[COUNSEL]` Define both inside the service schedule rather than incorporating a
published definition that can change silently. Require notice before a definition
changes.

`[BUILD]` Each check behind each state must be mechanically evaluated and its result
recorded, because the customer relies on the state to accept delivery. A state
asserted without evidence is a warranty with nothing behind it.

## The Existing Business Audit

For customers who already trade. Each area is classified Keep, Improve, Replace or
Missing.

`[COUNSEL]` The audit produces findings about a customer's existing setup that may
touch legal, tax, insurance or compliance matters. The contract must state that the
audit is an operational review, not a legal, tax, insurance or compliance audit, and
that a Missing or Replace verdict is not a determination of legal compliance.

This matters: telling a customer their insurance is "Missing" sounds like advice
about what insurance they need. It must be framed as an observation that an item is
absent, with a recommendation to consult a qualified professional.

`[BUILD]` Audit findings that touch regulated areas should carry an explicit
referral to a qualified professional rather than a bare verdict.

## Customer responsibilities

- Accurate information about the business and the founder
- Complete Founder Actions when prepared
- Hold their own licences, permits, insurance and registrations
- Obtain their own legal, tax and insurance advice where needed
- Approve third-party purchases before they are made
- Respond within agreed windows

`[COUNSEL]` Non-response by the customer stalls the build and the fee is already
paid. Define what happens: a hold period, then a defined outcome. Without it, the
engagement stays open indefinitely and the revenue is never recognised.

## Acceptance and completion

`[COUNSEL]` Define when the engagement is complete. Handoff is the natural point,
and handoff has a defined product meaning. Tie completion to handoff, and define
what happens if the customer never completes the Founder Actions needed to reach it.

## What is not included

Ongoing operation. That is Build & Run, which is optional and separately priced. The
site says this and it should be restated in the terms so that a customer who bought
the build does not believe operation continues.
