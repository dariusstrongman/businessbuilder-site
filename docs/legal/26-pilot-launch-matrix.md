# 26. Pilot launch matrix

> **NOT LEGAL ADVICE. NOT ATTORNEY REVIEWED.** Decision aid only.
> One page. Seven issues. Every row is an action.

## Verdict

**The pilot can go, on a narrow path.** Narrower than the current product scope.

| The path | |
|---|---|
| Customer | An **existing** cleaning company. No formation, no entity selection |
| Channel | **Email only.** No SMS, no voice, inbound and reply only |
| Signing | At a **fixed business location**, not the customer's premises |
| Sale | A negotiated services agreement, **not self-serve checkout** |
| Claims | No profit, earnings or customer-acquisition representation anywhere |

## 1. Federal business opportunity rule — COUNSEL REQUIRED

The only issue that can make the offer unsellable in its current form.

**Do now.** Sell the website-only offer. Run intake, research and recommendation, none of which is a sale. Sell the pilot as a negotiated agreement with a signature, not a checkout.

**Not yet.** Same-day self-serve purchase of Build my business or the bundle. Any claim that Business Builder brings, generates or provides customers.

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| "so you appear for the right searches" | "Service area, hours and categories set correctly" |
| "Start taking customers if you want to. Many founders do" | "You can start trading while the remaining work finishes" |
| "Inbound lead handling" as a headline benefit | "Your enquiry form, CRM and calendar, connected and tested" |
| Anything describing leads or bookings arriving | Describe what is built and verified, never what it produces |

**The discipline:** describe the systems, not the outcome.

- **Attorney before first payment: YES.** This is item one.
- **Checkout: changes if the rule applies.** The disclosure must precede payment by seven days, which no instant-purchase path can satisfy. Interim: no self-serve purchase for the business offers.
- **Build & Run scope: no change.** Its marketing must not promise customer acquisition.

---

## 2. Texas business opportunity act — GO, conditional

Needs **both** a profit representation and a marketing program. The marketing element is already satisfied. The profit element is absent and must stay absent.

**Do now.** Keep selling. The constraint is permanent, not a gate.

**Not yet.** Nothing is blocked. Any earnings language flips this into a filing-before-advertising regime plus a $25,000 bond.

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| "pays for itself" | "A published price, not a range" |
| "you'll book more jobs" | "The systems are built and verified. What the business earns is yours" |
| "typical customers see…" | Nothing. There are no typical customers |
| Any dollar figure of customer revenue | Omit entirely |

**Where this breaks: sales conversations.** The least controlled surface. One line on a call about how much a cleaning company usually makes trips it instantly. Brief anyone who speaks to a prospect.

- **Attorney before first payment:** confirm no representation exists. Not blocking.
- **Checkout: no change. Build & Run scope: no change.**

---

## 3. Home-solicitation cancellation — GO, one decision

**Do now.** Sign the pilot agreement **at a fixed business location**. The statute exempts it, and this is the whole fix.

**Not yet.** Do not sign at the cleaning company's premises without the statutory notice and a detachable duplicate cancellation form. **A non-compliant contract is void.**

If signing on site is unavoidable, the notice must be boldface, at least 10 points, adjacent to the signature line, in these words:

> YOU, THE BUYER, MAY CANCEL THIS TRANSACTION AT ANY TIME PRIOR TO MIDNIGHT OF THE THIRD BUSINESS DAY AFTER THE DATE OF THIS TRANSACTION. SEE THE ATTACHED NOTICE OF CANCELLATION FORM FOR AN EXPLANATION OF THIS RIGHT.

- **Attorney before first payment:** only if signing on site.
- **Checkout: no change. Build & Run scope: no change.**

---

## 4. SMS and voice — HOLD

**Do now.** Email only. Inbound handling, replies to messages the end customer started, booking confirmations by email, web form responses.

**Not yet.** Any outbound text. Any outbound call. Any AI voice answering. Including booking reminders.

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| "We'll text your customers" | "Replies by email, from your address" |
| "SMS reminders" | "Email confirmations and reminders" |
| "AI answers your phone" | Remove entirely |
| "Never miss a call" | "Every enquiry reaches one place" |

**Why the hold is firm.** A single non-consented text to a Texas mobile can be a federal violation, a violation of two Texas chapters, and a deceptive-practices claim with mandatory one-way fees. Scripts are filing-disclosable as public information, which means AI system prompts would be. And voiceprint capture carries $25,000 per violation.

- **Attorney before first payment:** not required if both are off. Required before either is ever enabled.
- **Checkout: no change.**
- **Build & Run scope: YES.** The pilot scope must exclude SMS and voice expressly, and the capability must be **disabled, not merely unused**. An unenforced capability is the same gap as an unenforced permission.

---

## 5. UPL and entity guidance — HOLD on selection, GO for the pilot

**The pilot is an existing business.** No formation, no entity selection, no EIN. That removes this issue from the pilot almost entirely, and it is the strongest argument for starting with an existing business rather than a new one.

**Do now.** Record the founder's own answers onto a form and file it. Publish templates that state clearly they are not a substitute for an attorney. Explain what a step is and why it exists. Track, connect, verify. Tell the customer when they need an attorney or an accountant.

**Not yet.** Select an entity type. Recommend one structure over another. Draft or customise an operating agreement. **Draft service or quote terms for a customer.** Answer "which is better for me". Touch the S-corporation election. Bundle a "free" operating agreement, which is the exact pattern the case law rejects.

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| "we **file** it against your company" | "we **record** it against your company" |
| "Drafted for your trade and attached where customers see them" | "Prepared from your instructions. Have your attorney review them before you rely on them" |
| "We created the account" | "We prepared the setup. You opened it and accepted the provider's terms" |
| "We identify what applies to your trade and location" | "We list what commonly applies. Confirm it with your authority or attorney" |
| "what your trade and contracts typically require" | "the cover types businesses in your trade are commonly asked for. What you need comes from your broker" |
| "We recommend an LLC" | "We explain the options. Choosing between them is yours and your attorney's" |

**The line:** the safe harbour covers designing, creating, publishing, distributing, displaying and selling. Not selecting, customising, applying or advising.

- **Attorney before first payment: YES for any business build.** Not for this pilot, if formation is out of scope.
- **Checkout: no change.**
- **Build & Run scope: YES.** The pilot must not draft the customer's service or quote terms. Use the customer's own, supplied by them.

---

## 6. Privacy and evidence handling — COUNSEL REQUIRED

**Do now.** Collect the minimum: business name, contact, service details, price rules. Tell the customer that people here review work before it goes out.

**Not yet.** Publish a privacy policy, because nothing supports one until the security work is verified. Claim encryption, access control or deletion timelines. Accept uploads containing identity documents. Touch any end-customer data before a written processing agreement exists.

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| "bank-level security" | Describe what is actually done, or say nothing |
| "we take your privacy seriously" | Delete. It signals a template |
| "your data is encrypted" | Only once verified |
| "delete any time" | "Ask us and we will delete it, with these exceptions" |
| "we never share your data" | Only if true of every subprocessor |

Add, once true: *"Please do not send identity documents, bank statements or tax numbers unless we ask for them."*

**Specific to this pilot:** home addresses combined with scheduled absences are the sensitive combination. Restrict access, exclude from general logs, return or delete at the end.

- **Attorney before first payment: YES**, plus a written processing agreement before any end-customer data is touched.
- **Checkout: YES.** A privacy notice must exist and be presented before the intake form collects anything.
- **Build & Run scope: YES** for the pilot, on address handling and no voice capture.

---

## 7. Build & Run cancellation — GO, with checkout work

**Do now.** Keep "stop any time" as published. No minimum term, no notice period. Cancellation by written request, acknowledged, effective at the end of the paid month.

**Not yet.** Claim self-service cancellation, automatic billing stop, or that operation halts, until each is built and verified.

| Avoid | Safer |
|---|---|
| "Cancel any time with one click" | "Email us and the plan ends at the end of the month you have paid for" |
| "No ongoing fee, ever" | "No Business Builder monthly fee" and state the hosting term separately |
| "Nothing is rebuilt" on return | Decide the reactivation fee first, then say it |

Keep, and make true: *"Stop whenever you want. The company stays yours."* And on the activation fee: *"Not refunded, because it bought setup you keep."*

- **Attorney before first payment:** recommended.
- **Checkout: YES, and this is the blocking piece.** Separate affirmative consent to the $299 recurring charge, not bundled into general terms. Full policy text presented, not linked. A version id of the policy persisted against the transaction.
- **Build & Run scope: no change**, but a verified halt path must exist before the pilot starts.
