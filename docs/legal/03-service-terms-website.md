# 3. Build My Website, service terms

> `[COUNSEL]` marks a decision a lawyer must make. `[BUILD]` marks a sentence that
> is not yet true in the product.

A service schedule under the master Terms of Service. It does not stand alone.

## Commercial terms

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Price | $795, one-time |
| Recurring | None |
| Pricing note | Founding customer pricing during the early launch period |
| Third-party costs | Separate. See [10-third-party-fees.md](10-third-party-fees.md) |

## Scope

What is included, taken from the current site copy, which now becomes contractual.

- Creative direction made for the customer's business, not chosen from a template
- Page structure and copy shaped around how the trade gets hired
- Quote and enquiry forms with the fields the work requires
- Designed and checked at phone, tablet and desktop widths
- Domain connected, HTTPS verified, analytics receiving
- Every path tested end to end before it is called live
- Full ownership, source files and export

`[COUNSEL]` Every line above is a promise. Review each for deliverability. Two need
attention:

**"Every path tested end to end before it is called live."** This is a strong
warranty of completeness. It needs a definition of "path" and of "tested", or it will
be read against Business Builder when any defect appears. Recommendation: define the
tested paths in the scope document for each build, so the promise is bounded by an
enumerated list rather than being open-ended.

**"Full ownership, source files and export."** Ambiguous as drafted. See the
deliverable definition below. This phrase currently appears on the site and must be
either defined or changed. See [20-website-copy-audit.md](20-website-copy-audit.md).

## Exclusions

Stated positively on the site as "the website only". The contract should enumerate.

Not included: company foundation, CRM, scheduling, payments, operations, ongoing
maintenance, content updates after delivery, hosting beyond initial connection,
ongoing SEO, advertising, or any Build & Run operation.

`[COUNSEL]` Decide the post-delivery position. A customer whose site breaks two
months later will ask. Options: nothing included, a defect window, or a paid
maintenance option. A short defect-correction window is conventional for custom
build work and is recommended. Set its length and define what counts as a defect as
opposed to a change.

## The deliverable, defined

The most important definition in this document.

> **Website Deliverable** means the source files for the website built for Customer
> under this Order, comprising the page templates, styles, content, configuration
> and assets specific to Customer's site, in a form that allows Customer or a
> third party engaged by Customer to host, modify and maintain the site
> independently of Business Builder.
>
> The Website Deliverable does not include the Business Builder website generation
> engine, the reusable component library in its general form, internal prompts, or
> any other Business Builder Technology, except as embedded in the Website
> Deliverable under the licence in [the IP clause].

`[COUNSEL]` Confirm this against [11-ip-ownership-model.md](11-ip-ownership-model.md).
The phrase "independently of Business Builder" is a functional commitment: the export
must actually work standalone. If the site requires a Business Builder service to
run, the commitment is false.

`[BUILD]` Verify that an exported site actually builds and runs without any Business
Builder dependency. If it does not, either the export must change or the promise
must. This is testable and must be tested before the first sale.

`[BUILD]` Third-party licence inventory for embedded fonts, images and libraries
must be produced at handoff.

## Acceptance

`[COUNSEL]` The scope needs an acceptance mechanism, which the current site copy
does not describe. Without one there is no defined moment when the work is done,
which makes both the refund policy and the revision limit unenforceable.

Recommended structure:

1. Business Builder delivers and notifies
2. Customer has a defined review window to identify anything not matching the
   agreed scope
3. Items in scope are corrected. Items outside scope are quoted as changes
4. Acceptance occurs on written approval or on expiry of the review window

`[COUNSEL]` Set the review window. Deemed acceptance on expiry is standard and
necessary, otherwise a silent customer leaves the engagement permanently open.

## Revisions and change control

`[COUNSEL]` Not currently defined anywhere and it must be. Unbounded revisions are
the most common way a fixed-price build becomes unprofitable, and the most common
source of agency disputes.

Recommended: a defined number of revision rounds within the agreed direction, with
changes to the direction itself handled as a change order at a published rate.

## Customer responsibilities

- Provide accurate business information, service descriptions and pricing
- Provide or approve content, and hold the rights to anything they supply
- Warrant that customer-supplied material does not infringe third-party rights
- Respond within the agreed windows
- Hold their own domain and hosting accounts

`[COUNSEL]` The customer-supplied content warranty and a matching indemnity are
standard and important. A customer who supplies an infringing photograph should bear
that risk.

## What is not warranted

Drafted to be specific rather than sweeping.

- No search ranking, traffic, lead volume, or revenue outcome is promised
- Third-party platforms and providers may change in ways that affect the site
- Continued operation after handoff depends on the customer maintaining their own
  hosting, domain and accounts
- Browser and device behaviour changes over time

`[COUNSEL]` The site says designed and checked at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
Confirm what browser and device support is actually committed, because "works
everywhere" is not deliverable and the commitment should be bounded.

## Interaction with other offers

Build my website is the narrowest offer. A customer who later buys Build my business
should not pay twice for the same work.

`[COUNSEL]` Define the upgrade credit. The site says each package contains the one
before it, which implies a customer who buys the website first and upgrades later is
not charged twice for the website. That implication is now a commitment and needs a
stated mechanism, including whether the credit expires.
