# Research and design rationale

Reviewed September 13, 2026. Independent implementation; no files or assets from another implementation were used.

## Product-site references

- [Linear homepage](https://linear.app/) and [pricing](https://linear.app/pricing): product-native detail makes a broad workflow tangible. The information architecture uses concrete stages and separate trust/control content. Desktop and 375px screenshots are saved under `artifacts/research/`. The first desktop capture caught its entrance animation; the mobile rendering and page content were also inspected. Borrowed the principle of precise workflow exposition, not its dark visual identity or layout.
- [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/payments): its hero places a specific customer transaction beside the offer. This supports making Business Builder's hero show a company deliverable and connected systems. Desktop and mobile captures were made; the desktop visual was inspected. Business Builder uses a warm paper surface and a distinct assembly composition.
- [Ramp approval documentation](https://support.ramp.com/vendor-approvals/) and [bill-pay approvals](https://support.ramp.com/bill-pay-approvals/): approvals are attached to specific events and responsibilities. Applied that principle to Founder Actions and operational boundaries. Ramp's public homepage returned a machine-readable representation in this browser, so no claim is made about a visual inspection of its actual homepage.
- [Lovable pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing): usage, building, and running have different implications. Business Builder's comparison explicitly separates the initial project from ongoing service, without inventing prices or presenting an arbitrary credit model.

## Competitive context

| Reference | Observed proposition | Implication for Business Builder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Durable](https://durable.com/) | Positions itself as an AI business builder, with website, CRM, bookings, reviews, and business assistance. | “More than a website” is insufficient differentiation. Make evidence and founder handoff visible. |
| [Polsia](https://polsia.com/) | Broad company operations across planning, code, advertising, and customer communication. | Explain the limits of ongoing operations and make permissions a product feature. |
| [Pulsia](https://pulsia.ai/) | French digital-strategy and AI systems offer for small businesses. Browser rendering and page text were captured. | It is distinct from Polsia. Coordinated operating systems are already a competitive proposition. |
| [Audos](https://audos.com/) | Describing an idea is the starting point for AI building, running, and growing a business. Browser content also discusses personalized agents and support. | Do not claim the describe/build/run narrative is unique. Differentiate with service-business scope and inspectable completion. |
| [CoFounder.AI](https://cofounder.ai/) | Contextual business partner with specialist delegation and staged founder onboarding. | Founder context matters before recommendation. Show what is learned, approved, and retained. |
| [Ori / Cofounder](https://cofounder.run/) | An AI cofounder that performs business tasks. | “Cofounder” is an ambiguous competitive name; do not treat similarly named products as one company. |
| [Denovo](https://www.denovo.dev/about) | Create, launch, and operate across the whole business journey. | End-to-end breadth is contested. Completion criteria and ownership need to be specific. |
| [ZenBusiness](https://secure.www.zenbusiness.com/pricing-products/) | Business formation and associated start/manage services. | Distinguish business assembly from entity formation; make founder and professional responsibilities explicit. |
| [Tailor Brands](https://www.tailorbrands.com/about) | Business services spanning setup, management, and growth. | A collection of tools is not the key story. Explain the coordinated work and deliverable. |
| [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/pricing) | Conversational web software creation and ongoing usage. | Keep the founder's outcome central, rather than asking customers to manage generation credits. |

These are observations about public positioning, not independent verification of competitors' capabilities or quality. No competitor statistics, testimonials, or customer claims are reused on the homepage.

## Selected direction

The Company, Assembled: warm paper, ink, cobalt action color, restrained green verification surfaces. Manrope and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted. The homepage reveals a company assembly, a twelve-stage progression, a Build Room, supported operating examples, an evidence receipt, readiness definitions, a handoff choice, scope comparison, and trust answers.

The UI illustrations are native HTML/CSS and semantic SVG marks. They are explicitly illustrative. There are no stock photographs, fake testimonials, invented customer outcomes, fabricated prices, or live integration claims.

## Research limits

This is focused product and visual reference research, not a comprehensive competitive feature audit. Interaction behavior on reference sites was not exhaustively tested. The site does not infer verified capabilities from competitor marketing language.
